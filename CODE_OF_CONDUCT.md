# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AgentAudit a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors should:

- communicate respectfully and constructively;
- focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people;
- welcome reasonable questions and differing technical perspectives;
- respect privacy and confidentiality; and
- accept responsibility, apologise when appropriate, and correct mistakes.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the project maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and promptly as reasonably possible.

## Attribution

This policy is intentionally concise and is informed by the principles of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
